Active nitrogen flux measurement during GaN
growth based on the transmitted signal detected
with a pyrometer

                     Matteo Canciani, Stefano Vichi, Oksana Koplak, Sergio
                     Bietti and Stefano Sanguinetti

                           University of Milan-Bicocca, via R. Cozzi, 55, 20126, Milan (Italy)
                           E-mail: stefano.vichi@unimib.it

                           December, 2024

                           Abstract. A novel approach for the measurement of the Nitrogen active species
                           generated by a plasma source in the molecular beam epitaxy environment is here
                           presented. The method is based on the analysis of the variations in the optical
                           signal measured by a pyrometer during a two step, Gallium rich and Nitrogen
                           controlled, growth modes. The method permits a precise, quantitative and direct
                           measurement of the flux of active species as a function of the plasma generation
                           parameters of the cell: nitrogen gas flux and RF-power.

Keywords: GaN, Pyrometer, Molecular Beam Epitaxy, Calibrations
